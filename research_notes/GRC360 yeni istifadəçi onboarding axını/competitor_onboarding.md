# Competitor onboarding: how GRC and compliance-automation products take a new customer from first login to first value

Research date: 2026-10-14. These notes cover 15 products. Most sources are vendor help centers, vendor service descriptions or statements of work (SOWs), and review sites. Each claim notes when its source is third-party or vendor marketing. Coverage varies a lot by product: Drata, Secureframe, Vanta, LogicGate, AuditBoard and SimpleRisk have detailed primary sources, while Thoropass, Archer and MetricStream have thin ones.

## Q1. What the first run looks like and the order of setup steps

### Takeaway
The compliance-automation tools (Drata, Secureframe, Vanta, Sprinto) all use the same pattern. The admin sees a checklist that stays in the product and shows a progress bar. The steps run in this order: company profile, then identity provider and integrations, then framework scope, then control owners, then personnel, then policies. Risk, vendors and auditor come later. The enterprise platforms (AuditBoard, LogicGate, ServiceNow, Archer, MetricStream, OneTrust) have no real self-serve first-run flow, because a services team configures them before users arrive. The open-source tools (Eramba, SimpleRisk, CISO Assistant) give an install or superuser wizard and then an empty application. Eramba users have complained about this for years.

### Cited Findings

**Drata (most detailed public first-run flow)**
- The Quick Start is a guided checklist for Admins only. Customers who joined on or after Feb 24, 2026 get the "New Experience" version by default. It does not have to be finished in one session, and it shows "100% completion" once all required activities are done. — [Drata Quick Start (New Experience)](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/13265650-quick-start-guide-new-experience)
- The sections run in this order. (1) **Provide Basic Info**: the company info fields on the Org Info tab, which auditors and monitoring tests use. (2) **Make Connections to Power Automation**: identity provider (IdP) first, because other steps depend on it, then Infrastructure, Version control and Ticketing. (3) **Establish Continuous Compliance for Your Frameworks**: assign roles (optional, needs the IdP), scope the framework requirements on the Frameworks page, then review gaps and plan remediation on the Controls page (assign owners, approvers, scope). (4) **Ensure Your Personnel Stays Compliant**: HRIS (optional), verify employment status, internal security settings (workstation evidence, security training, visitor logs), background checks. (5) **Set Up Your Policies** in the Policy Center (creation, approval, acknowledgement). A "What's Next" list then suggests vulnerability management, observability integrations and Trust Center. — [Drata Quick Start (New Experience)](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/13265650-quick-start-guide-new-experience)
- The classic Quick Start shows locked activities (with a lock icon, meaning a prerequisite is missing) and skippable activities, plus a progress count per section. It also has an extra step, "Onboard your personnel to Drata", which invites in-scope users to install the agent, complete training and upload evidence. It warns that policy completion can take "a few days to a few weeks". — [Drata Quick Start (classic)](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/12004296-quick-start-guide); [New Experience article, classic differences](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/13265650-quick-start-guide-new-experience)
- Drata also publishes a recorded 60-minute onboarding webinar that tours Quick Start, Frameworks, Controls, Monitoring and policy management. — [Drata onboarding webinar](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/12401592-drata-onboarding-webinar-recording)

**Secureframe**
- A "Getting Started" checklist sits in the top left, and the dashboard shows its progress bar. It covers connecting integrations, publishing policies and inviting the team. The integration items are cloud resources (AWS, Azure, GCP), devices (Jamf, Kandji, CrowdStrike) and personnel (Okta or an HRIS). — [Secureframe: Getting started](https://support.secureframe.com/hc/en-us/articles/17355197856915-Getting-started-with-Secureframe)
- Onboarding has three phases. **Phase 1, account setup and access:** accept the invite, complete the company profile, invite other admins and stakeholders, explore the dashboard and checklist. **Phase 2, integrations, frameworks and program setup:** connect cloud, HR, device and identity tools, prioritize a framework, assign test and control owners, add policies, training and background checks, and start automated evidence collection. **Phase 3, operationalizing and audit readiness:** framework scoping and risk assessments, failed tests, connecting an auditor, and optionally a Trust Center and Knowledge Base. — [Secureframe onboarding overview: roles, milestones, best practices](https://support.secureframe.com/en/articles/15111050-secureframe-onboarding-overview-roles-milestones-and-best-practices)
- Secureframe defines "Onboarded" as a list of milestones: personnel invited and categorized (in or out of scope); policies published; training assigned; a background check provider chosen; at least 1–2 integrations syncing (for example AWS, Okta, Gusto); **vendor list and risk register populated**; control and test owners set; auditor selected and evidence review begun. — [Secureframe onboarding overview](https://support.secureframe.com/en/articles/15111050-secureframe-onboarding-overview-roles-milestones-and-best-practices)
- Secureframe also offers guided onboarding videos on integrations, personnel setup and starting a compliance program. — [Secureframe guided onboarding videos](https://support.secureframe.com/en/articles/15111048-getting-started-with-secureframe-using-guided-onboarding-videos)

**Vanta**
- The **Compliance Roadmap** opens from the profile icon. It "walks you through the product and outlines the steps needed to use Vanta effectively", hides itself three days after completion, and can be reopened. A "?" menu links to the Help Center, Community, Product Feedback and **Live Training**. The article does not list the Roadmap's steps. — [Vanta: Getting Started Resources](https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345375-getting-started-resources-in-vanta)
- Vanta has a **Sandbox Mode** and a set of guides that includes "Bringing your Current Compliance Program into Vanta", "Best Practices: Migrating Your Security Program to Vanta", "Adaptive Framework Scoping", "Getting Started with Workspaces", "Getting Started: Personnel Hub" and a "Change Management Guide" (internal rollout). — [Vanta Getting Started Guides collection](https://help.vanta.com/en/collections/12575234-getting-started-guides)
- Vanta's GRC Implementation Guide sets out the phases. **Onboarding (weeks 1–4):** kick-off call; confirm the scoping approach (workspaces or segmentation by framework); review existing controls, policies and risks; configure user access and notifications. **Program Import & Setup (months 1–3):** integrations, controls and frameworks, policies, risks, vulnerabilities, personnel onboarding, access reviews, vendors. **Internal Assurance & Audit Prep (months 3–6):** fix failing tests, upload evidence, finalize risk treatment, schedule the audit. **Audit Navigation:** run the audit in Audit View. — [Vanta GRC Implementation Guide](https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11488007-vanta-grc-implementation-guide)

**Sprinto**
- Sprinto's FAQ says "most customers complete the core configuration within a few hours", with extra time for policy approvals, training assignments and vendor assessments. It gives no fixed order. Frameworks are switched on or off on the Frameworks page and **billing depends on which frameworks are enabled**. Staff come from integrations, bulk upload or manual entry. Policies can be uploaded, taken from templates, or written in an editor. Training can be the built-in campaigns or an external provider. Devices are covered by the Dr. Sprinto agent or an existing mobile device management (MDM) tool. Integrations can be added at any time. — [Sprinto FAQ](https://docs.sprinto.com/frequently-asked-questions-faqs)
- A customer review describes onboarding as taking "one morning", "with the wizard walking us through every control". This is one reviewer's experience, not a benchmark. — [Sprinto reviews on AWS Marketplace (G2 syndicated)](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/reviews/reviews-list/prodview-ixyb464cbjkam?page=27)

**Hyperproof**
- The fastest way to create a program is to add a framework from the template library. Administrators and Compliance managers can do this. — [Hyperproof docs: Creating a program by adding a framework](https://docs.hyperproof.io/user/en/getting-started/creating-a-program-with-illustrative-controls)
- Users are invited from Settings > People and given an organizational role. An invited user **cannot be assigned to objects until they accept** the invitation. Bulk onboarding works through SCIM with Okta, which needs SSO. — [Hyperproof docs: adding users](https://docs.hyperproof.io/cm/en/get-to-know-hyperproof/manage-users/adding-users-to-your-organization)
- The help center has a "Getting started with Hyperproof" collection of 40 articles and a "Training" collection of 138 articles. — [Hyperproof Help Center](https://intercom.help/hyperproofio/en/)

**ServiceNow IRM (Policy and Compliance Management)**
- The docs give a "setup checklist" of mandatory and optional steps. The mandatory sequence is: assign roles (sn_compliance.admin, sn_compliance.manager), set application properties, create policies, create control objectives linked to policies, create attestations. Optional steps follow. — [ServiceNow docs: setup checklist for GRC Policy and Compliance Management](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/DDKNGuzcS_gt6P_P4KM3Dg/3fvMwuiqQeQQmayK~mntzA)
- Partner AC3 advises spending time on entity design first, then starting small and iterating. — [AC3: GRC in ServiceNow IRM](https://www.ac3.com.au/resources/governance-risk-and-compliance-in-service-now-s-irm)

**Open source: Eramba, SimpleRisk, CISO Assistant**
- **Eramba:** On first SaaS access, the admin sets an email and password. The post-install course then covers email (SMTP) settings, time zone, Enterprise licence key, branding and translations. — [Eramba SaaS Installation course](https://www.eramba.org/learning/courses/99); [Eramba VMware install course](https://www.eramba.org/learning/courses/13/episodes/275)
- **Eramba:** A forum thread from 2019 asked for a checklist-style "get started" wizard and a fictitious demo company. The user said they did not know what to do after mapping business units. Eramba replied that users should go through the documentation "from top to bottom" and pointed to trainings. In June 2026 Eramba said its training material had been revamped and pointed to eramba.org/learning and a free five-day training. The thread never says a wizard or demo data was built. — [Eramba forum: missing step-by-step wizard](https://discussions.eramba.org/t/question-missing-step-by-step-wizard-to-start/1170)
- **SimpleRisk:** A six-step browser installer runs once: Welcome, Health Check, Database Credentials, SimpleRisk Configuration (database name and user, default language, database-backed sessions), Admin Account Creation, Installation Execution. The last step creates the database, loads a schema that "seeds initial reference data", creates the admin and registers the instance, then redirects to login. The article does not say whether demo data is included. — [SimpleRisk: The initial configuration wizard](https://support.simplerisk.com/kb/01-05-the-initial-configuration-wizard)
- **SimpleRisk:** The first risk is added from Risk Management → Submit Risk. Only the Subject is mandatory. A public demo at demo.simplerisk.it uses a restricted account. — [SimpleRisk: How to submit a risk](https://support.simplerisk.com/kb/how-to-submit-a-risk-in-simplerisk); [SimpleRisk user guides](https://support.simplerisk.com/kb/simplerisk-user-guides)
- **CISO Assistant:** The quick start is a free cloud trial, or `docker-compose.sh` followed by creating a superuser. Frameworks are loaded from **Governance → Library → Load** and can be loaded straight from Excel. The README describes no guided tour or sample data. — [CISO Assistant README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/intuitem/ciso-assistant-community@main/README.md)
- **CISO Assistant:** A third-party "ciso-assistant-bootstrap" skill for AI agents, listed under intuitem, guides first setup in this order: create a folder (domain) for the organization, then a perimeter (for example "Production Environment") and its assets, then import a framework such as ISO 27001:2022, then create a compliance assessment on that perimeter. It suggests a 3x3 risk matrix for a quick start. This is not official product documentation. — [ciso-assistant-bootstrap skill](https://smithery.ai/skills/intuitem/ciso-assistant-bootstrap)

### Inferences
- The automation tools agree on an order: **org profile → IdP/SSO and integrations → choose and scope frameworks → assign control owners → personnel (invite, policies, training) → policies → risks and vendors → auditor.** Risk register and vendor inventory come late (Secureframe Phase 3, Vanta months 1–3) because these products are built around controls and evidence. A broader GRC product like GRC360 may want to bring risk and assets in earlier.
- Drata's lock-and-skip behaviour (some steps need the IdP first, some are optional) and Vanta's Roadmap hiding itself after completion are cheap design patterns worth copying.
- Eramba's long-running "where do I start?" complaint shows the cost of shipping an empty multi-module GRC with no wizard. GRC360 covers similarly broad modules, so it should not repeat this.

### Gaps
- Vanta's exact Compliance Roadmap steps and in-app wizard screens are not publicly documented in text. The help article does not list them.
- I found no public first-run documentation for Thoropass, AuditBoard, LogicGate, OneTrust, Archer or MetricStream. Their onboarding happens inside services engagements.
- For ServiceNow, I could not confirm whether the in-instance "Guided Setup" module for IRM in current releases differs from the docs checklist.

## Q2. Pre-loaded frameworks and content, and how framework selection shapes what the customer sees

### Takeaway
Every automation vendor ships framework libraries with controls already mapped, plus policy templates and training. Choosing a framework creates the requirements, the mapped controls, the automated tests and the required policies, and these together form the readiness view the admin then works through. The enterprise platforms ship prebuilt "applications" or content subscriptions (UCF) that services teams then configure. The open-source tools ship framework libraries that the user imports by hand.

### Cited Findings
- **Vanta:** "We have 15 policy templates to help you get SOC 2 compliant", many of which are reused for ISO 27001. A Policy Builder guides drafting for SOC 2 and ISO 27001:2022. A framework is audit-ready only when its policies are approved and employees have accepted them. — [Vanta: Getting started with policies](https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345363-getting-started-with-policies)
- **Vanta** claims more than 35 frameworks. Its pre-built CIS Controls framework has "over 150 controls … 170 tests, and 90 documents" (2025 press release). — [YC company page](https://ycombinator.com/companies/vanta); [SDxCentral press release](https://www.sdxcentral.com/press-releases/vanta-introduces-new-capabilities-to-help-security-teams-improve-their-posture-and-reduce-risk/)
- **Vanta** describes training made by Vanta as a source of value ("Vanta's pre-made trainings for our employees", G2 reviewer). — [G2 Vanta reviews](https://g2.com/products/vanta/reviews?page=10)
- **Drata:** Each enabled framework appears as a card with readiness, completed and remaining requirements, and mapped control counts. Policy templates are "developed by Drata's GRC team with input from auditors". **Archiving a policy template removes its controls from the readiness score.** — [Drata Quick Start (New Experience)](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/13265650-quick-start-guide-new-experience)
- **Sprinto:** Frameworks are enabled per framework, which also drives billing. It has policy templates and built-in training campaigns, and vendor due diligence applies only to vendors rated high risk by the configured criteria. — [Sprinto FAQ](https://docs.sprinto.com/frequently-asked-questions-faqs)
- **Hyperproof:** Programs are created from predefined framework templates. A 2026 release note says all new and updated frameworks "include Controls and are fully crosswalked to the Hyperproof Crosswalk dataset". Older frameworks did not always include controls. — [Hyperproof docs](https://docs.hyperproof.io/user/en/getting-started/creating-a-program-with-illustrative-controls); [Hyperproof release notes 2026-06-17](https://intercom.help/hyperproofio/en/articles/15483429-release-notes-2026-jun-17)
- **Hyperproof** markets "Jumpstart" for mapping existing controls across frameworks, and also supports custom frameworks. — [Hyperproof: create your own framework](https://hyperproof.io/create-your-own-framework/)
- **LogicGate:** Each implementation package covers one prebuilt "Application", configured from a pre-built template. Its older product overview names IT Risk Management, Compliance Management, Enterprise Risk Management and Third-Party Risk Management (2020 source). Standards and Regulations content updates come with Success plans. — [LogicGate services description](https://www.logicgate.ai/services-description); [eWEEK overview, 2020](https://www.eweek.com/de/it-management/logicgate-risk-cloud-product-overview-and-insight/)
- **ServiceNow IRM** can subscribe to the Unified Compliance Framework (UCF) to pull in controls and authority documents. — [AC3](https://www.ac3.com.au/resources/governance-risk-and-compliance-in-service-now-s-irm)
- **CISO Assistant:** The README claims "+220 frameworks included", but its own numbered list shows 130 built-in plus 38 community frameworks, so the count is inconsistent. Libraries also include threat catalogs and reference controls, and can be loaded from Excel. — [CISO Assistant README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/intuitem/ciso-assistant-community@main/README.md)
- **Vanta's AWS integration** alone is said to automate evidence for "approximately thirty to forty percent of SOC 2 security controls". This is a third-party estimate. — [Agency blog: Vanta + AWS setup](https://blog.getagency.com/articles/vanta-aws-integration-complete-setup-guide)

### Inferences
- Selecting a framework is the main driver of what the product shows. It fills in requirements, controls, tests, required policies and the readiness score, so after setup the product behaves like a to-do list built from the framework. Drata ties archived policy templates directly to the score, which shows how tightly content and progress are linked.
- For GRC360, the equivalent is to make framework or library selection an early wizard step and seed controls, policy templates and a risk catalog from it. CISO Assistant's library model (threats plus reference controls plus frameworks, importable from Excel) is the closest open-source reference.

### Gaps
- No public source gives exact counts of policy templates, controls or risk-library entries for Drata, Secureframe, Sprinto or Thoropass.
- I could not confirm whether Vanta or Drata pre-load a risk scenario library during onboarding. Vanta's implementation guide mentions "new risk scenarios" but not a pre-loaded library.
- AuditBoard's, OneTrust's and MetricStream's pre-built content (control or risk libraries) was not documented in the sources I could reach.

## Q3. Human help: kickoff, CSM, packages, and implementation timelines

### Takeaway
The automation vendors pair self-serve checklists with a CSM or compliance expert. Their typical time to "onboarded" is about 2–8 weeks, and to audit-ready about 3–6 months. The enterprise GRC vendors sell phased professional-services packages (discovery, design, configure, test, launch) that run about 1–5 months per module or application, with kickoff meetings and customer homework. Their total programs often run 2–4+ months, and some partner estimates reach 8–16 weeks for a first ServiceNow module.

### Cited Findings
- **Secureframe timeline:** initial setup 1–3 days; integrations and owner assignment 1–2 weeks; policy and personnel setup 1–3 weeks; risk, vendor and control review 2–4 weeks. Total **30–60 days**, record **under 7 days**, "many customers complete onboarding in 30 days with a dedicated Admin". Support comes through in-app chat (Mon–Fri) and a CSM by email. — [Secureframe onboarding overview](https://support.secureframe.com/en/articles/15111050-secureframe-onboarding-overview-roles-milestones-and-best-practices)
- **Secureframe** roles: Admin/Project Lead, IT/Security (integrations and technical tests), HR/People Ops (personnel, training, background checks), Executive Approver (policies, scope). — [same](https://support.secureframe.com/en/articles/15111050-secureframe-onboarding-overview-roles-milestones-and-best-practices)
- **Vanta:** The kick-off call is "the first meeting between the customer and Vanta" to agree roles, scope and goals, and the customized GRC implementation plan is shared there. It uses a 30-60-90 plan: day 30 onboarding and planning, day 60 core buildout and first passing tests, day 90 operational and audit-ready, month 6 audit. The CSM helps align auditor request lists with tests. — [Vanta GRC Implementation Guide](https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11488007-vanta-grc-implementation-guide)
- **Sprinto (vendor claim):** "4–6 weeks to go live", with a dedicated Compliance Success Manager for every customer and onboarding "guided by ISO-certified auditors". — [Sprinto vs Secureframe page](https://go.sprinto.com/lp-secureframe-alternative); [Sprinto vs Delve page](https://go.sprinto.com/delve-vs-sprinto)
- **Drata** offers onboarding sessions and a recorded onboarding webinar. — [Drata FAQ](https://drata.com/resources/faq); [Drata webinar](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/12401592-drata-onboarding-webinar-recording)
- **Thoropass:** Reviewers praise hands-on guidance ("walk through the process step by step") and a "checklist of action items and examples". Others found the start overwhelming because "there are a lot of tasks in the program". Its audit firm is in-house (Thoropass Assurance, a CPA firm under common ownership) and issues the SOC report. These points come from third-party review summaries. — [soc2auditors.org Thoropass review](https://soc2auditors.org/insights/thoropass-review/); [Sprinto's Thoropass review (competitor source)](https://sprinto.com/blog/thoropass-review/)
- **AuditBoard (Customer Advisory Services SOW, Feb 2025, Module Upgrade package):** **Initiation** 1–2 weeks (kickoff, timeline; customer names a primary contact and **completes AuditBoard Academy online training**). **Discovery** 1–2 weeks (customer explains its company, processes and data). **Design** 2–4 weeks (design examples built in production with the customer's data, up to 2 design sessions, the customer gives feedback within 5 business days). **Configure** about 2 weeks (roles and teams, data migration, user accounts, an optional sandbox copy). **Test** 2–4 weeks (activate users, site review, the customer tests). Custom dashboards, integrations and historical data migration are excluded unless listed. — [AuditBoard Implementation SOW, Module Upgrade 2025-02-26 (PDF)](https://optro.ai/download/agreements/implementation/archive/AuditBoard-Implementation-Services-Module-Upgrade-02262025.pdf). This package applies to existing customers upgrading modules. The Sep 2025 Permission and Adoption Workshop packages list Initiation about 1 week, Discovery about 1 week and Design 2–3 weeks. — [AuditBoard Permission Package 09152025](https://optro.ai/download/agreements/implementation/archive/AuditBoard-Implementation-Services-Permission-Package-09152025.pdf)
- **LogicGate Risk Cloud** packages, one Application each: **Lite about 30 days**, Basic about 60, Standard about 90, Premium about 120, Advanced about 135, Elite about 150 days (Elite adds change-management guidance). Every package includes requirements work, setup, data import and admin training. Only Premium adds end-user training. Success tiers: Standard (Help Center, LogicGate Learning portal, chat); Premier (6 requests per month plus Power User training); Technical Account Manager tiers with 90 or 180 Professional Services hours per year, project management and onsite visits. — [LogicGate services description](https://www.logicgate.ai/services-description)
- **LogicGate** implementation staff own "kickoff, process design, build, training, and rollout" and then hand off to Customer Success (from a job posting). — [Built In job posting](https://builtin.com/job/implementations-services-associate/3784219)
- **OneTrust:** The vendor's G-Cloud listing says "typically implemented within one to two months". A PeerSpot reviewer estimated about 3 weeks. G2 (German page, Privacy Automation) shows an implementation time of 3 months. Reviewers mention a steep learning curve, and a competitor review (Sprinto, so possibly biased) says teams "spend weeks in configuration before seeing any real value". — [G-Cloud listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/660488860974899); [PeerSpot](https://peerspot.com/products/onetrust-grc-reviews/page-2); [G2 DE](https://www.g2.com/de/products/onetrust-privacy-automation/reviews?page=4); [Sprinto OneTrust review](https://sprinto.com/blog/onetrust-review/)
- **ServiceNow IRM (partner estimates):** 8–16 weeks for the first module, 4–8 weeks for each additional module (BDEMerson). Risktec offers "8–10 weeks" for Risk Management. Data migration of policies, risks, controls, audits and findings is a major driver. — [BDEMerson](https://www.bdemerson.com/service/servicenow-grc-implementation); [Risktec](https://risktec.tuv.com/?p=93068); [Workable job post](https://apply.workable.com/tawantech/jobs/view/7DE9F9A872.md)
- **Archer:** Partner phases are current-state analysis, roadmap, configuration, integrations, then bulk data load. No public duration was found. Common starting use cases are ERM, TPRM and Issues Management. — [AWS Marketplace partner listing](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-h4s7lztdgbfq2); [Archer services](https://www.archerirm.com/de/services)
- **MetricStream:** No public duration. Gartner Peer Insights reviewers report over-promised deliverables that "delayed the (phased) implementation several times". The audit product scores 3.2 for Integration & Deployment, its lowest subscore. — [Gartner Peer Insights, MetricStream Audit Management](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/audit-management-solutions/vendor/metricstream/product/metricstream-audit-management); [Sprinto MetricStream review (competitor)](https://sprinto.com/blog/metricstream-review/)
- **Eramba:** Free online trainings (historically 2 hours per day for a week, now a "five-day training") and paid onsite trainings. Eramba does not support community users with install questions. — [Eramba forum](https://discussions.eramba.org/t/question-missing-step-by-step-wizard-to-start/1170); [Eramba Community](https://eramba.org/get-community)

### Inferences
- There are two service models. The automation vendors include a CSM in the subscription and measure success by milestones such as first passing test or first approved policy. The enterprise vendors sell scoped SOW packages per module, with design sessions and acceptance testing.
- Even the self-serve leaders put the realistic time at 30–90 days, mostly spent on policy approval and remediation, not configuration. The in-product checklist gets through the configuration part in hours or days.

### Gaps
- No public kickoff or CSM details for Hyperproof or Thoropass beyond reviews.
- Vanta's own self-serve setup time (as opposed to the full GRC program) is not published.

## Q4. How progress is shown and how non-admin users are onboarded

### Takeaway
Progress appears at three levels: checklist completion (a % or progress bar), per-framework readiness (requirements met and controls passing), and per-person compliance status (policies accepted, training done, device agent, background check). Non-admins are onboarded mostly through invitations and assigned tasks: control and test owners get notified of failures, and employees get a personal task list (accept policies, complete training, install agent). Auditors get a separate role or view.

### Cited Findings
- **Drata:** Quick Start % complete and per-section counts. Framework cards show readiness, requirements completed versus remaining, and controls mapped. The Personnel page shows each person's compliance status with reset, reminder and exclusion actions. — [Drata Quick Start (New Experience)](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/13265650-quick-start-guide-new-experience)
- **Drata:** "Onboard your personnel" invites in-scope users to log in for the agent, training and evidence uploads. — [Drata classic Quick Start](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/12004296-quick-start-guide)
- **Secureframe:** The admin dashboard shows onboarding progress, test health, integration status, personnel activity and action items. Admins categorize personnel (contractor or full-time, in or out of scope), publish policies to individuals or groups, assign training, start background checks, and assign test and control owners. Test status can be overridden with a justification. — [Secureframe onboarding overview](https://support.secureframe.com/en/articles/15111050-secureframe-onboarding-overview-roles-milestones-and-best-practices)
- **Secureframe** has a separate guide for employees completing their own onboarding. — [Secureframe: Completing employee onboarding](https://support.secureframe.com/en/articles/15900492-completing-employee-onboarding-employee-guide)
- **Vanta:** Owners are assigned per hub (controls, policies, risks) and per test. Owners are notified of failures in Vanta or Jira. Personnel tasks are assigned by group. Audit View shows outstanding evidence and owners. Slack and Teams reminders are available. Auditors get the "Auditor" permission. — [Vanta GRC Implementation Guide](https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11488007-vanta-grc-implementation-guide)
- **Vanta:** Policies must be approved and **accepted by employees** before the company counts as audit-ready. — [Vanta: Getting started with policies](https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345363-getting-started-with-policies)
- **Sprinto:** The onboarding workflow tracks completion of assigned policies, trainings and device compliance, and reminders can be sent from the dashboard. — [Sprinto FAQ](https://docs.sprinto.com/frequently-asked-questions-faqs)
- **Hyperproof:** Users must accept the invitation before they can be assigned objects. SCIM through Okta brings in users without individual invites. — [Hyperproof docs](https://docs.hyperproof.io/cm/en/get-to-know-hyperproof/manage-users/adding-users-to-your-organization)
- **ServiceNow:** Attestations are the mechanism for collecting input from control owners. — [ServiceNow docs](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/DDKNGuzcS_gt6P_P4KM3Dg/3fvMwuiqQeQQmayK~mntzA)
- **AuditBoard and LogicGate:** End users are activated near the end of implementation (AuditBoard's Test phase). LogicGate includes end-user training only in its Premium package and above. — [AuditBoard SOW](https://optro.ai/download/agreements/implementation/archive/AuditBoard-Implementation-Services-Module-Upgrade-02262025.pdf); [LogicGate services](https://www.logicgate.ai/services-description)
- **Vanta** reviewers complain about waiting for integrations to poll, and about integrations that don't tell clients apart from employees, which forces repeated de-scoping. — [G2/AWS review aggregation via search](https://g2.com/products/vanta/reviews?page=5)

### Inferences
- A good pattern for GRC360 is (a) an admin setup checklist with %, (b) per-framework or program readiness, and (c) a "My tasks" inbox for each role (control owner, risk owner, employee, auditor). The employee track should be policy acceptance plus training plus a short checklist.
- Invite gating matters. Hyperproof's rule that users must accept before they can be assigned slows owner assignment. Drata gets around this by syncing people from the IdP first.

### Gaps
- I found no detail on how risk owners specifically are onboarded (risk-assessment tasks or emails) in any vendor's public docs.
- I could not verify the onboarding flows for auditor portals in OneTrust, Archer or MetricStream.

## Q5. Compliance-automation tools compared with enterprise IRM/GRC platforms

### Takeaway
Compliance-automation products (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Sprinto, Thoropass) give a self-serve checklist driven by the chosen framework, with a CSM. Configuration takes hours or days, onboarding about 30–60 days and audit readiness about 3–6 months. Enterprise IRM platforms (AuditBoard, LogicGate, ServiceNow, Archer, MetricStream, OneTrust) are configured by professional services per module or application, over about 30–150 days per application or 8–16+ weeks per first module, with design sessions, data migration and UAT before most users ever log in. Open-source tools give install wizards and libraries but leave program design to the user.

### Cited Findings
- **Fast side:** Sprinto says core configuration takes "a few hours". Secureframe says 30–60 days to onboarded, with a record under 7 days. Vanta's plan is 90 days to audit-ready and 6 months to audit. — [Sprinto FAQ](https://docs.sprinto.com/frequently-asked-questions-faqs); [Secureframe](https://support.secureframe.com/en/articles/15111050-secureframe-onboarding-overview-roles-milestones-and-best-practices); [Vanta](https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11488007-vanta-grc-implementation-guide)
- **Slow side:** LogicGate packages run 30–150 days per Application. AuditBoard's phases total roughly 8–14 weeks for a module (my sum of the SOW ranges). ServiceNow is 8–16 weeks for the first module according to a partner. OneTrust is 1–3 months by vendor, G2 and reviewer figures. MetricStream reviewers report delays. — sources above in Q3
- **Thoropass** combines the platform with an in-house auditor, so onboarding leads straight into its own audit. Reviewers say this suits companies new to SOC 2 but not those whose policy requires an independent auditor. — [soc2auditors.org](https://soc2auditors.org/insights/thoropass-review/)
- **Archer** transformations are "often described as multi-year". A single migration post covered only Phase 1, over six months. This is a weak, single source. — [LinkedIn profile post](https://fi.linkedin.com/in/seppo-ryynanen-21812a8b)

### Inferences
- GRC360 sits between the two groups: it covers broad GRC modules but is a multi-tenant SaaS. A likely best fit is automation-style self-serve onboarding (wizard → framework and library seeding → checklist with % → role-based task inboxes) with an optional assisted track (kickoff, data-import templates, per-module setup) for larger tenants. This is a design inference, not a sourced finding.

### Gaps
- No independent benchmark compares time-to-first-value across vendors. Most timelines come from vendors or partners.
- The Archer and MetricStream figures rest on weak or anecdotal sources.
